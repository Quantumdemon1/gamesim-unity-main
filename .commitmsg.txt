Strategy windows: a word with whoever decides, decisions that listen, reply cards

Batch 4 of the minigames brainstorm's plan: lobbying the Head of Household
and the veto holder, reply cards, and two deal fixes (StrategyRules.cs,
ReplyCards.cs).

The windows: before nominations the player can reach the Head of
Household; before the veto meeting, the veto holder and the Head of
Household (about a replacement). A word costs one of the week's
conversations. Everybody else is still busy, and the station says who is
deciding.

Pleas copy the reference's veto lobbying: four approaches with its bases,
trait fits and rounding; four answers that move the decision 40-60, 15,
-5 or -30 to -50. Extended to the Head of Household (keep me off the block,
put somebody up) and to keeping the nominations as they are. A plea made
with a deal that lands writes a safety agreement through next week.

Decisions that listen: nominations, replacements and the veto now weigh
deals (the reference server's weights), alliances and this week's pleas as
well as warmth. A season without the windows reads warmth alone.

Deal fixes: no veto commitments once the veto is decided; nominees ask the
player veto holder straight after the competition, answered on the
decision screen; an accepted alliance invitation forms or joins an
alliance.

Reply cards: a confrontation, gossip found out (3 in 10) and a nominee's
plea each put a card up with the reference's three answers, made real:
relationship, memory, gossip back reaching the listener, and a promise of
support that is a vote promise.

Saves: schema 15 (strategyRulesStartWeek, lobbies, replyCards). New
seasons open the windows; migrated saves and the fixture seasons do not.
FrozenEpisodeV14 freezes the Have-Not contract.

Port verification's season walk answers reply cards with the middle
answer; the look sheet answers them before its house-event shot.
