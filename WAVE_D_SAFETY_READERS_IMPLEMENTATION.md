## Every material change

- New `UnifiedCommitmentHistory.cs`: detached, sorted canonical records and
  immutable grouped breach incidents. Every evidence ID remains attached to its
  actual nomination/replacement decision, settlement week, actor and wronged
  party. Exact length-delimited keys are verified against those stored parties;
  arbitrary, conflated, padded or trailing identities are refused. One strongest
  source relationship-effect owner is selected, then stable ID. This is not the
  full enabled-save validator, and does not mutate state, RNG or knowledge.
- `UnifiedCommitments.cs`: exposes the existing source consequence calculation
  internally so historical readers and verdict generation share the owner rule.
- `Story/StoryConsumers.cs`: canonical active Safety promises/deals reach the
  story's word readers. Unilateral promises do not give reverse consent. Story's
  own thirty-point term retains its story-version/start-week gate; version0
  retains its existing promise-versus-pact policy.
- `EpisodeEngine.cs`: actual combined nomination takes the maximum of story's
  Safety term and strategy's strongest hold, rather than adding the same shield.
  Other story, strategy and agency terms retain their independent effects.
- `ThreatAssessment.cs`: canonical promise/deal evidence counts once per breach
  incident, against its actor, using the existing capped reputation scale. Legacy
  promise counts remain unchanged. This is an explicit new-rule extension, not
  an overlapping legacy-season outcome claim.
- `WebJuryVoting.cs`: reads canonical pair obligations without mutable mirrors.
  One active Safety deal term; one strongest kept-deal reward per settlement week;
  one breach term from the selected source owner. Accepted-offer provenance keeps
  its heavier source weight; the wronged party is not blamed. Other families and
  the source's total clamp remain unchanged.
- `FinalistRead.cs`: the player's own Safety breach history counts incidents,
  not every promise/deal evidence row; no third-party knowledge is disclosed.
- `EpisodeEngine.UnifiedSafety.cs`, the source settlement routines in
  `EpisodeEngine.cs`, `Story/EpisodeEngine.StoryHooks.cs`, `Story/Grudges.cs`, and
  `ThreatAssessment.cs`: explicit internal pre-current-decision history context
  prevents a newly published canonical verdict from amplifying its own grudge.
  Every current decision incident is excluded only during that source grudge
  calculation; previous incidents still count and ordinary public threat reads
  include the settled history. Existing settlement method signatures remain as
  wrappers, preserving their call/reflection contracts. No transient save field,
  broad reputation suppression, new RNG draw or public UI override was added.
- New `UnifiedSafetyReaderTests.cs`:53 actual authored cases cover the composite
  nomination, gates, direction/status, old additive policy, grouped effects,
  exact corrupt-key refusals, immutable projections, source jury weights, kept
  rewards, wronged/third-party attribution, and one/two-nominee settlement history
  with a prior incident. Two explicit new GUIDs are unique, including ignored
  assets. Pure registration and floors raised to3815Edit/988Play/77UMA/2581pure.
  Future combined integration must retain QA11Edit/4Play:3826/992/77/2581.
