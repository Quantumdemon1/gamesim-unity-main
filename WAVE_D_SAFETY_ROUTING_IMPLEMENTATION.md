## Every material change

- `EpisodeEngine.cs`: Safety promises and accepted player deals use canonical
  storage; proposals preflight before the acceptance roll. Pending NPC Safety
  responses resolve stable IDs and update only their actual owner, preserving
  proposal week and late-answer term. Main nomination, replacement-only, final
  veto and promise-week-turn boundaries call the Safety gateway. Other families
  remain legacy-owned; old-rule branches preserve their original flow.
- `EpisodeEngine.HoHPitches.cs`: an actual pitch reply supplies HoHPitch provenance
  to the promise writer, retaining free replies and existing reaffirmation.
  `EpisodeEngine.Commitments.cs`: ordinary departure routes through the existing
  end-with-evictee boundary after reconciliation, not at the earlier status change.
- `UnifiedCommitmentStore.cs`: read-only source-shaped deal preflight and a narrow
  true-owner canonical linked-price expiry. Refusals publish no draft; a price
  lapse is not a breach, effect, RNG draw or additional settlement receipt.
- `NpcPromises.cs`, `NpcDeals.cs`, `PlayerDeals.cs`: NPC promise/deal/offer creation
  and pending/binding/capacity readers see canonical Safety; no safety mirrors.
  Retained history in both storage owners counts toward source capacities.
  Manual new-rule Safety proposals use exact-duty/term duplicate checks, allowing
  genuine later terms rather than rejecting every arrangement of the same type.
- `EpisodeEngine.Strategy.cs`: actual Lobby safety writes use their source term
  through next week and count canonical history. Capacity/term preflight occurs
  before rolls/costs if a new safety bargain might be created. Non-safety vote
  lobbying remains in its existing store.
- `EpisodeEngine.Negotiation.cs`, `Negotiation.cs`: mixed accepted counters reserve
  both records atomically; links and call-in promises resolve stable IDs across
  actual owners. Voided prices mutate canonical or legacy ownership, never a read
  projection. Canonical promise restraint uses maximum hold rather than summing
  overlapping words. Source costs, consent, keyed counters and call-in gates stay
  with their existing command owners.
- `Story/EpisodeEngine.StoryEffects.cs`: story Safety promise/deal creation uses
  canonical ownership and advances its sequence only after successful storage.
  `Story/EpisodeEngine.StoryHooks.cs`: actual production expulsion has a distinct
  canonical expiry boundary.
- New `EpisodeEngine.UnifiedSafety.cs`: command-owned nomination, final-veto and
  expiry gateways validate affected source policies, identities and reciprocal
  links before canonical publication. All overlapping breach evidence receives
  one shared incident key; only the strongest source owner supplies consequences
  and its witness procedure. Existing source effect routines receive detached
  ACTIVE pre-settlement DTOs, not mutable mirrors or already-broken DTOs. Every
  affected bought record can still void its true-owned price exactly once.
- `UnifiedCommitments.cs`: appends Expulsion without changing previous enum
  ordinals. Ordinary departure expires deal-origin Safety only; expulsion expires
  both promise/deal families. Promise turn and deal-pass expiry remain separate.
  New-rule overlapping final-veto deals receive one strongest positive reward per
  spared partner, all eligible records fulfilled; promises never fulfill there.
  This positive deduplication is a deliberate new rule, not overlapping legacy
  outcome parity. Previously nominated/veto-saved contestants are not "spared."
- `StrategyRules.cs`: nomination reluctance reads strongest canonical Safety
  protection once. Non-safety weights, target pulls and other terms remain.
- Four new test classes add130 authored cases:31 command-owner,37 other-writer,
  33 resolution and29 protection. Reflection reaches the real bounded internal
  routines without exposing public UI writers. Tests distinguish prospective
