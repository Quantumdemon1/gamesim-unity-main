## 2. Rules every slice keeps

1. **Captions are contracts.** Tests and screen readers find controls by caption. "Space  Speed up   ·   Enter  Skip", "Click anywhere to continue", "Season report", "Start a new season", "Review the season", "Main menu", "Watch finale replay", "The jury's questions", "Go to episode screen", "Vote to evict {name}" and every progress line ("Revealing vote N of M", "One key left", "N keys. Two will not get one.", "Nominated for eviction") keep their words. The named children tests find ("Nominee", "HoH", "Progress", "Controls", "Card glass", the "Key " prefix, "Ballot", "Subject name") keep their names; new children never start with "Key ". New captions are unique among live controls.
2. **The knowledge gate, now with ballots in it.** Nothing during play shows hidden scores, true sentiment, NPC-to-NPC private events, or - new - a houseguest's ballot the player has not learned. The tally, the HoH's tie-break and the player's own ballot are public; everything else is known only by the player's own inference from the count, by what a houseguest told them (which may be a lie), by what they overheard, or by what an ally reported. The same gate holds for NPCs once wave B lands: a houseguest reacts to a ballot they know, not to the box.
3. **Recorded seasons do not move.** Hiding a ballot, re-sourcing a reader or redrawing a card changes no roll and no outcome, so waves 0 and A are presentation and knowledge only. Every change to an outcome - what an NPC does with a ballot they learned, a lie about a cast ballot, a new verb - goes behind a rules start week, `ballotRulesStartWeek`, with its own schema (23; 22 is the commitments' from ACTIONS-DEALS-ALLIANCES-PLAN), claimed with the story session. New command kinds are appended after `LockFinalArgument`.
4. **Presentation first, one definition of a ballot.** One reader, `KnownBallots`, says what a ballot looks like to the player (tally, own, tie-break, proven by the count, told, overheard, reported); every screen, recap, page and judge reads it, never `state.votes` or the public line. The engine's `vote-reveal` event becomes per-voter private; the public record gets one tally line.
5. **Layout.** Label boxes at least 1.3 times their font (Inter draws nothing under 1.21x). Layouts hold at FontScale 1.0 and 1.2, on the 16:9 frame and the 4:3 batch canvas, for houses of 3 to 16 and juries of up to 14. A label measured in a tree rebuilt while inactive is measured wrong: activate before measuring, or measure on LateUpdate. World-space text hides under screen-space boards and panels. Every visible change is checked by a capture, and the ceremony captures are taken at the frame the defect showed (a second play of the card, the Shot framing).
6. **The kit.** The pack's sprites go in as `Pack9_CeremonyFinale` under Resources/Packs by the same route as Pack 8 (an installer script, the catalogue, UiPackImportTests' counts, PackArt names, Unity's metas from a D: import). Cards draw with HudPrimitives and UiTheme on the ceremony frames and EndScreenKit on the stage, as today; the nomination mark is the key, the vote's and the keys' faces are ring discs, the controls line is one size on every screen frame.
7. **Tests.** Each defect slice starts with a test that fails on main. Each slice raises the floors in its merge commit. Behaviour that changes the audited walk is fixed in the walk in the same slice: the walk asserts one public line per ballot at the reveal (PortVerification.Blocs.cs:85-91) and must assert the tally line and the private ones instead.
