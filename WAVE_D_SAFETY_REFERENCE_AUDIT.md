## Knowledge policy and actual hearing effects

`Knowledge.BrokenWord` is currently deal-shaped/reference-keyed; `Knowledge.Spread`
processes facts independently and `StorySystemsAt` applies hearing effects to each
returned fact/listener. Grouping YourWord's page is not actual hearing deduplication.
New-model spread needs incident/listener coordination and explicit audible evidence,
without inventing witnesses or additional penalty/RNG procedures.

The existing source promise routine intentionally emits no deal-style BrokenWord
fact. `UnifiedSafetyResolutionTests` explicitly retains that source distinction:
promise-owner zero facts versus deal-owner one. A unified reputation policy that
adds promise knowledge is a declared native extension, not isolated-source parity.
Keep source leaf evidence intact and make any composite change explicit, versioned
and separately tested; do not silently weaken the existing source assertions.

  authority; a hand-authored opportunity fixture is not this validation.

  scoring validation. Summary Term/Speech defenses do not certify a corrupt save.

  native command transaction/cancel/stale-control/save-failure/reload coverage.
